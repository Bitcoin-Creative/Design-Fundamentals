Base Script for the Project

Act 1: The Risks We See

    SCENE 1: Close-up on the distressed face of a driver inside a dented car, in front of a repair shop.

        NARRATION: "You have insurance for your car..."

    SCENE 2: A chef works with focus and precision in a professional kitchen.

        NARRATION: "...for your property..."

    SCENE 3: A security camera on the wall of a house monitors a street at night.

        NARRATION: "...for your family's health..."

    SCENE 4: A patient is wheeled down a hospital corridor in a wheelchair, seen from behind.

        NARRATION: "...for your life."

    SCENE 5: A young person holds a cellphone with a completely shattered screen.

        NARRATION: "Maybe even for your phone."

Act 2: The Risk We Ignore

    SCENE 6: Extreme close-up of an eye, reflecting the word "CENSORED" in red.

        NARRATION (Sharper tone): "But nothing to protect you from the government's actions?"

    SCENE 7: A serious-looking politician in his office, with the Brazilian flag in the background, in front of an envelope of cash on the table.

        NARRATION: "Nothing to protect you from the choices of others?"

    SCENE 8: Close-up of the sweaty, determined face of a construction worker under the sun. Then, a transition to an exhausted nurse during her shift.

        NARRATION: "You trade your time, your energy, your life... for money."

    SCENE 9: A woman in a supermarket aisle looks with concern at a product on the shelf, pondering the purchase.

        NARRATION: "And while the value of your effort... evaporates."

    SCENE 10: A hand approaches a keyboard in front of a monitor with the Central Bank logo. The scene suggests the act of creating money digitally.

        NARRATION: "...They create that same money for themselves, with the push of a button."

Act 3: The Awakening and the Solution

    SCENE 11: A family stands at their front door looking at an eviction notice.

        "Inflation is worse than you think..."

    SCENE 12: A husband playfully interacts with his pregnant wife's belly.

        NARRATION: "And Bitcoin is better than you imagine."
        "The cycle is always the same."

    SCENE 13: Animation of planet Earth from space as the text: CORRUPT MONEY -> CORRUPT INCENTIVES -> CORRUPT SOCIETY revolves around it, representing the cyclical and universal nature of the issue.

        NARRATION: "Corrupt money. Corrupt incentives. Corrupt society."

    SCENE 14: Black screen. The Bitcoin logo appears, shattering the cycle which breaks apart visually.

        "Break the cycle."

    SCENE 15: The same planet, now being enveloped by an orange liquid.
    (The "Bitcoin bubble," which will cover the world).

        NARRATION: "Own Bitcoin.
        Your monetary insurance."

    SCENE 16:

    TEXT ON SCREEN: Bitcoin. Your monetary insurance.

    The End.
